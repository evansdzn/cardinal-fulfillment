Common questions

Straight answers, including the ones about price

If yours isn't here, ask in the quote form and we'll answer it in writing.

How much does 3PL fulfillment cost per order?

3PL fulfillment costs at Cardinal start at $3.15 per order for pick and pack, plus postage and $24 per pallet monthly storage. Most Columbus clients shipping 1,000–5,000 orders land between $4.50 and $7.00 all-in. Answer five questions and we'll send your exact per-order pricing, not a range.

Do you have a minimum order volume?

No. Cardinal is a 3PL with no minimum order volume. We onboarded a brand shipping 400 orders a month in 2024; they now ship 3,100. If you're outgrowing your spare room, you're big enough. Smaller accounts get the same named account manager as everyone else.

How long does 3PL onboarding take?

3PL onboarding at Cardinal takes 14 days from signed agreement to first shipment. Most providers take six to eight weeks. The timeline depends on how clean your SKU data is and how quickly inventory reaches Groveport. Our fastest pre-Q4 migration went live in nine days.

What happens if you lose or damage my inventory?

Cardinal carries $10M in cargo and warehouse legal liability insurance, separate from general liability. Lost or damaged inventory is reimbursed at your landed cost, filed through claims@cardinalfulfillment.com and settled within 30 days. Every SKU is counted and photographed at receiving, so discrepancies surface before they become disputes.

Do you handle returns and exchanges?

Yes. Returns processing is included in our flat fee schedule. We inspect every return, restock sellable items within 48 hours, and quarantine damaged stock for your decision. Cardinal integrates with Loop Returns and Returnly, so refunds and return labels trigger automatically without anyone emailing us.

Can you do subscription boxes and kitting?

Yes. Cardinal handles subscription boxes and custom kitting across our Groveport and Obetz buildings. We ship 2,400 recurring boxes monthly for one coffee client, kitted and dispatched on a fixed date each cycle. Inserts, branded packaging and seasonal variations are quoted per kit, never added as surprises.
